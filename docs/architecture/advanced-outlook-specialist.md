# Advanced Outlook Specialist

The Outlook specialist begins only after confirmed device resolution and granted diagnostic consent. It detects `outlook.exe` as classic Outlook and `olk.exe` as new Outlook before selecting any client-specific diagnostic. Classic COM add-in, profile, OST/PST, ScanPST, search-index, and safe-mode assumptions are prohibited for new Outlook.

Incident-class plans use only the four governed read-only capabilities and at most eight diagnostic steps. Conditional RAG queries carry tenant, client, Outlook build, Windows version, incident class, and permission filters. Retrieved guidance cannot override endpoint evidence, consent, policy, or tool boundaries.

Every hypothesis requires evidence identifiers. Weak evidence requests clarification; close strong hypotheses preserve contradiction for evidence fusion. Remediation remains a proposal with risk, qualified approver, pre-state, rollback for persistent changes, technical checks such as send/receive, and employee confirmation. Service, identity, network, unsupported-build, and data-loss risks escalate to their qualified owners.
