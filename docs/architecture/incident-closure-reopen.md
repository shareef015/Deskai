# Incident Closure and Reopen

Closure requires every technical and regression check, explicit employee confirmation, a privacy-safe resolution summary and evidence references. The resulting record is immutable and includes a deterministic SLA outcome and closure fingerprint.

A knowledge candidate is merely queued for separate human review; closing an incident never publishes reusable guidance automatically.

Reopen preserves the original closure and requires a governed recurrence or regression reason plus new evidence. The audit export links conversation, evidence, decisions, execution, rollback, verification, human custody, closure and reopen events through hashed actor and event fingerprints.
