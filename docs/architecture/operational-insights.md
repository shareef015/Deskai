# Operational insights dashboards

Operational charts consume typed, tenant-scoped snapshots that cannot mix live and synthetic data. Windows are limited to 90 days, series and point counts are bounded, units are metric-specific, percentages are range checked, and deterministic ordering yields replayable fingerprints.

Each chart has a concise operational question, visible freshness, textual takeaway and expandable data table. Counts use a zero baseline; symbols and labels accompany color; reduced-motion and forced-color preferences inherit the shared accessibility contract.
