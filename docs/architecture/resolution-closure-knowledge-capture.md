# Resolution, Closure, and Knowledge Capture

Closure is a proof gate, not a conversational claim. The record requires a grounded root cause, critic-passed plan, human approval, bound execution result, technical and business-function verification, authenticated employee confirmation, evidence references, and the complete immutable provenance chain. Missing or malformed inputs fail closed.

The resolution summary links the cause and verified change without copying raw telemetry or employee content. Secret-like values and email addresses are redacted, length is bounded, and only evidence identifiers enter the closure record. The audit map preserves root-cause, plan, approval, execution, and verification fingerprints.

A reusable knowledge candidate contains a redacted problem pattern, grounded resolution, domain, and evidence references. It remains pending human review and cannot be published by the agent. Recurrence never reuses the earlier closure as proof; it reopens a new diagnostic cycle with the previous verification lineage intact.
