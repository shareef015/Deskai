# Synthetic conversations and employee responses

The conversation generator binds every generated Outlook, printer, scanner, and Windows/network incident to a deterministic employee journey. Each transcript begins with a time-aware greeting and help question, then records intake, clarification, classification, device selection, scoped diagnostic consent, evidence review, distinct remediation approval, verification, employee confirmation, and closure.

Five deterministic branches exercise approval, diagnostic-consent decline, remediation rejection, failed verification, resolution, monitoring, and escalation. Consent is never inferred or reused as approval. Declining connection prevents diagnostic turns, rejecting remediation prevents execution, and resolution is impossible without explicit employee confirmation.

All people, endpoints, answers, timestamps, and decisions are synthetic. Transcripts contain no mailbox or document content, credentials, or customer data and are regenerated exactly from stable case seeds.
