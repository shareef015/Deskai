# Synthetic workforce

The fictional demo workforce extends the authority personas with stable reporting relationships, office assignments, support groups, domain skills and shifts. One executive root anchors the reporting tree; every other person has a valid manager. Support coverage spans Outlook, printing, scanning, Windows/network, endpoint, identity and security domains.

Skills are routing metadata only and never grant authorization. Effective permissions still come from the governed role model. All people and assignments carry the synthetic tenant identifier, and deterministic regeneration must reproduce the committed fixture byte for byte.
