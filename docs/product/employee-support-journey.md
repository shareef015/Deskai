# End-to-End Employee Support Journey

DeskPilot begins with a time-aware greeting and asks how it can help. It gathers
only missing information, identifies the employee and assigned device, and
explains why endpoint access is needed before requesting diagnostic consent.

Read-only diagnostics produce an evidence timeline. The system ranks root-cause
hypotheses, exposes uncertainty, and proposes the smallest reversible repair.
Authorization is action-specific and risk-aware. Execution records pre-state,
result, post-state and rollback evidence.

The original business function is then tested. DeskPilot asks the employee to
confirm the result and closes only after both technical verification and human
confirmation. Declined consent, unsupported work, expired approval, failed
verification and retry exhaustion lead to safe cancellation or evidence-rich
escalation.
